# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ufazien to be a project anyone can take part in, whether they are students, first-time contributors or experienced developers, without harassment or fear of being belittled.

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, particularly with people who are new
- Accepting constructive feedback without treating it as an attack
- Giving feedback about the work, not the person
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and moving on
- Assuming the other person meant well

Behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and code review, and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to **fuadelizade6@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They may remove or edit comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain why when they do.

The privacy and safety of whoever reports an incident will be respected.

### Consequences

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning explaining what was wrong and why, possibly with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a period of no interaction with those involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from participation.
